Our mission

The sky is always
telling a story.

We bring together observers, theorists, and data scientists to understand the brief, brilliant events that reshape our universe.

Our team combines rapid-response observations, archival discovery, precision cosmology, and new computational tools. Working across the electromagnetic spectrum, we turn moments of cosmic change into lasting insight.

Selected research

Tracking the
transient universe.

Our programs span nearby stellar explosions, distant gravitational lenses, dusty environments, and intelligent discovery at survey scale.

01 / Hubble

Narrow-Field NUV Transient Survey

The deepest and fastest ultraviolet time-domain survey of its kind, designed to catch the shock breakout and cooling of core-collapse supernovae.

This is NUTS!

02 / Cosmology

Lensed Supernovae

Using time delays between gravitationally lensed images to probe cosmic expansion and dark energy.

03 / Webb

Dusty SNe with JWST

Distinguishing newly formed ejecta dust from pre-existing material around supernovae.

04 / Big Data

Machine Learning for Transients

Building tools that find the rarest and most informative events among thousands of nightly discoveries.
